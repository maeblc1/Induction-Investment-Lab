# Data provenance and limitations — 7 November 2026

* SNB: `snb_verified_observation.csv` holds **one independently verified** spot yield on 10-year Swiss Confederation bonds, **0.450% on 2026-11-06**, reported by the Swiss National Bank at https://www.snb.ch/en/the-snb/mandates-goals/statistics/statistics-pub/current_interest_exchange_rates . Unit: percentage points per annum. One observation cannot support a historical volatility estimate. The official SNB data portal offers historical series at https://data.snb.ch/en .
* ElCom-derived C3 files: `tariffs_C3_clean.csv` (18,277 rows, 2020–2027; columns `year`, `kanton`, `gemeindeNummer`, `gemeindeName`, `netzbetreiber`, `kategorieName`, `energie`, `netznutzung`, `abgaben`, `netzzuschlag`, `variable_tariff`, `total_reported`, `messwesen`, `n_operators`) and `tariffs_C3_summary_by_year.csv` (eight annual rows; `n_rows`, `median_variable_tariff`, `p10`, `p90`, `log_change_pct`) were **supplied by the user on 2026-11-07**, not directly downloaded or independently authenticated against ElCom. The original download/extraction date and transformation script were not provided. The app labels these as user-supplied ElCom-derived data, not independently verified official observations. Official provider: https://www.elcom.admin.ch/en/tariff-data-and-visualisations . Unit for `variable_tariff`: Rp/kWh. Annual national medians are constructed **first taking a median per municipality/year across multiple operators, then the median across municipalities**. The 2027 entries are for a future tariff year; they are not realised consumption observations. C3 is a standard profile rather than restaurant-specific marginal/avoidable cost. Neither `variable_tariff` nor any other total should be assumed fully avoidable: use the tariff-share adjustment or the user's own marginal electricity cost.

* Annual rate/growth volatility is *not* calibrated unless a user supplies a multi-year dataset (>=5 annual observations). The model's uncertainty sliders remain user-assumed scenarios otherwise. Calibration assumes annual independent growth and yield observations, which must be justified separately.
* Observed monitored kWh saving (18,000 per annum) and CHF 25,000 estimated equipment price referenced in `model.PRESETS` come from the **SFOE report's case study and modelling assumptions respectively**, not quotations for the user's own restaurant. Supplier quote and energy audit recommended.
* A nominal discount rate must be paired with nominal cash-flow growth. The 2.5 percentage point spread is an unverified scenario input, not a researched SME risk premium.

## Bundled annual Swiss Confederation bond yields (added 7 November 2026)

- File: `data/yields_10y_annual.csv` supplied by the user. Seven annual rows for 2020–2026, columns `year`, `mean`, `last`, `min`, `max`, `n_obs`. `mean`, `last`, `min` and `max` are in **percentage points per annum**, NOT decimal yields. `n_obs` counts underlying daily observations; for 2026 it is 217 (partial year).
- Default risk-free rate uses the `last` entry for the latest provided year (2026: **0.505%**). The user can select year and annual mean vs last; the application can also take a manually entered rate. Last means final observation *in the user-supplied dataset*, not necessarily today's live rate.
- The prior one-day independently documented SNB snapshot `snb_verified_observation.csv` shows **0.450% on 2026-11-06**. This is **not identical** to the supplied 2026 annual-summary `last` of 0.505%; these observations cannot be reconciled without the dates and raw records underlying the annual CSV. Both are retained, separately identified, and never presented as interchangeable.
- The original SNB download URL/cube ID, data extraction and retrieval date for the annual series are not included with this upload. Independent verification against the daily SNB series remains pending. Official source portal: https://data.snb.ch/en .
- For historical volatility calibration, `calibrated_sds` uses the standard deviation (ddof=1) of the seven annual percentage yields and divides by 100 to produce a decimal-rate SD. This is a descriptive historical calibration, NOT a statistical forecast of the restaurant discount rate or proof of a +2.5pp risk spread. Annual observations are heterogeneous and 2026 is incomplete.
